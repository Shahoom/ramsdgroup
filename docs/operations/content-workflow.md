# Content workflow

1. An editor creates or updates both Arabic and English variants in `/admin/content`.
2. Slugs, titles, excerpts, body, SEO title, and SEO description are required/reviewed for both locales.
3. Editors save as `draft` or `review`; only `owner` and `admin` roles publish.
4. Public media requires Arabic and English alt text. Private exports never belong in the public bucket.
5. Before publishing a claim, metric, credential, testimonial, or case outcome, store its evidence and obtain approval. Set `claims_verified` only after that review.
6. Publishing revalidates both locales. Confirm the canonical, alternate locale, visible heading, and social preview after release.

Initial editorial topics are in the fallback repository. They are educational, avoid legal promises, and should be reviewed by the appropriate company specialist before production publication.
